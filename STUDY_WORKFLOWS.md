# Study workflows

## Topic guide

Search the relevant subject's transcripts and notes, reconcile both sources, explain the topic, and finish with cited key takeaways and open questions.

## Lecture summary

Use one lecture transcript plus its neighboring notes. Produce concepts, definitions, worked ideas, likely questions, and citations to timestamps/pages/slides.

## Flashcards

Generate atomic question-answer pairs. Each answer must include a source citation. Avoid cards unsupported by the source corpus.

## Practice questions

Generate a balanced set of recall, application, and synthesis questions. Store answers separately and cite evidence.
