# Citation rules

Use citations that a person can open and verify:

- Transcript: `[Subject — filename.txt — 00:38:12]`
- PDF: `[Subject — filename.pdf — page 12]`
- Slides: `[Subject — filename.pptx — slide 8]`
- Notebook: `[Subject — filename.ipynb — cell 5]`
- DOCX/text: `[Subject — filename.docx — paragraph 14]`

Never invent a page, slide, cell, paragraph, or timestamp.
